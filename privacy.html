Privacy Policy for Hex Battles

This privacy policy applies to the Hex Battles mobile application (the “Application”), published by Spilcafeen ApS (the “Service Provider”) as a free service. It describes what the Application does and does not do with your information.

Short version

Hex Battles collects nothing. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it makes no network requests of its own. It does not need an internet connection to play. Nothing you do in the game leaves your device.

Information the Application collects

None. The Application does not collect, transmit or share any personal information, device identifier, contact detail, usage statistic or location data.

The Application does not ask you to create an account or provide any information about yourself, and there is no server for it to send information to.

Information stored on your device

The Application saves two things in your device's private application storage so the game works the way you expect between sessions:

  • your settings and menu choices, such as map size, number of opponents, difficulty, colour and which game elements are switched on;
  • your game in progress, so that you can resume it later.

This data stays on your device. It is not readable by other applications, it is never uploaded, and the Service Provider has no access to it. Uninstalling the Application deletes it. You can also clear it from within Android, under Settings → Apps → Hex Battles → Storage → Clear data.

Location

The Application has no location features. It never asks you for access to your location, and it does not store or transmit location data. Versions before 1.1.0 declared the Android location permissions because of a software library the game did not use; that library has been removed, and the permissions are gone from version 1.1.0 onwards.

Artificial intelligence

The computer opponents in Hex Battles are ordinary game logic that runs entirely on your device. The Application does not send your data to any artificial-intelligence service and does not use your data to train anything.

Third parties

The Service Provider does not share your information with anyone, because none is collected.

The Application is distributed through Google Play and runs on top of Google Play Services, which is part of the Android system rather than part of the Application. Google may collect information in connection with distributing and running applications, independently of the Service Provider. See the Google Privacy Policy for what Google does with that information.

Children

The Application is suitable for general audiences and collects no information from anyone, including children. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, because it does not collect personal information at all. If you are a parent or guardian with a question about this, please contact the Service Provider at the address below.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and equivalent laws you have rights of access, correction, erasure and portability over personal data held about you. The Service Provider holds no personal data from the Application, so there is nothing to access, correct, erase or export. The data described under “Information stored on your device” is under your control and is removed when you clear the Application's data or uninstall it.

Security

Because no information is transmitted or stored outside your device, there is no data in transit to intercept and no server-side store to breach. Data saved on your device is protected by Android's per-application sandbox.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any change will be published on this page with a new effective date. You are advised to consult this page if you want to know the current terms.

Contact

If you have any question about privacy in Hex Battles, contact Spilcafeen ApS at ht@spilcafeen.dk.
